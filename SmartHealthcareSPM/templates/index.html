5. Requirements Management

Add, update, prioritize and approve project requirements. New requirements begin with Pending approval.

Requirement Approval Rule

Rule: Only requirements with an Approved status can be included in the approved project scope. Requirements with Pending or Rejected status must remain excluded from the approved scope.

  • Approved Eligible for inclusion in the approved project scope.
  • Pending Excluded until approval is granted.
  • Rejected Excluded from the approved project scope.
